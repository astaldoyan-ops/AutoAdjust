Project: AutoAdjustPDRegulator
MCU: STM32F446RETx (рекомендовано) / STM32F103C8Tx
Purpose: Control analog PD regulator via digital potentiometers over SPI for automatic P,D tuning

Peripherals:
- SPI1 (Master) - MCP4251
- ADC1 - feedback measurement
- USART2 - debug
- TIM2 - timing/auto-tune
- GPIO - chip selects

Pins (Nucleo-F446RE):
SPI1_SCK  PA5
SPI1_MISO PA6
SPI1_MOSI PA7
CS_P      PA4
CS_D      PB0
ADC_IN    PA0
USART_TX  PA2
USART_RX  PA3
